# The roster

Status: exploration, 2026-09-23. A roster of famous civs and leaders with names players can say, covering
the whole world. It replaces the 72-civ exploration (in git history at `b71a4e9`), which went too niche.
Abilities are design sketches, not balanced numbers.

## Rules

1. **Famous first.** Each civ and leader should be known to a general player from school, films or other
   games. Recognition beats novelty. It's fine that Civ has used many of the same leaders.
2. **Easy to say.** Use the familiar English form: Montezuma, Genghis Khan, Suleiman, Canute. Any name
   that isn't obvious gets a pronunciation hint on its card and in the tables below.
3. **Popular civ name, leader sets the period.** The civ keeps its popular name (Aztecs, Vikings, China).
   The chosen leader fixes the period: city names, units, dress and scene all come from that leader's
   time. That keeps the accuracy without obscure polity names. The card can give the self-name
   (Mexica, Abbasid Caliphate).
4. **Actual rulers.** Every leader ran a state, in name or in practice. No religious founders. None
   whose reign falls mostly in the 20th century.
5. **The whole world, through famous names.** Every inhabited continent, drawn from the best-known
   figures of each region.
6. **Abilities from history.** Each ability comes from what the civ or leader is known for, and avoids
   stereotypes.

## What this gives up, and how to get some of it back

- **Women:** 4 of 24 first leaders (Cleopatra, Elizabeth, Catherine, Isabella). There are 7 once the
  second leaders are counted (plus Wu Zetian, Victoria and Seondeok). The later 16 add Maria Theresa,
  Nzinga and the Trưng Sisters. For more, make Wu Zetian China's first leader: she is well known and
  her name is easy to say.
- **Regions:** Europe has 7 of 24. The rest is Africa 4, West Asia 4, East and South Asia 5, and the
  Americas 4. Oceania waits until Hawaiʻi in the later 16.
- **Originality:** famous leaders overlap with Civ's rosters. Originality comes from the rewritten
  abilities, text, era-true names and the leader encounters instead.
- **Sensitivity:**
  - **Isabella:** her reign includes the Inquisition and the expulsion of Spain's Jews.
  - **Montezuma:** the Spanish conquest of his empire.
  - **Russia:** the current war.
  - **Abilities:** none of them celebrates conquest or slavery. The ability text says so plainly.

## Existing leader work

Most of the leader studio work already fits. Cleopatra, Genghis Khan, Elizabeth I, Ashoka, Alexander,
Cyrus, Mansa Musa and Wu Zetian are in the roster; Augustus becomes Rome's second leader. Marcus
Aurelius and Pericles move to the bench.

## Bench

Marcus Aurelius, Pericles, Hatshepsut, Saladin, Akbar's Mughals as their own civ, Tokugawa Japan as its
own civ, Kublai's Yuan, Haile Selassie (excluded as 20th century), Hiawatha (a founder figure; consult
first).

## Language note

Leaders speak their own language with subtitles. Where the language is ancient (Egyptian, Akkadian,
Old Persian, Classic Mayan), use one rule for all: either a scholarly reconstruction that the card says
is one, or the nearest living language.
