# Roster

The nations of civ.game, who leads them and what voice they speak. Agreed 2026-09-23.

## Rules

1. **Leaders ruled.** Heads of state, regents who held power, or elected magistrates who led the state. No Gandhi, Bismarck, Nobunaga, Gajah Mada; no legendary founders.
2. **Famous and admired.** Prefer rulers remembered as good at the job, including ones rarely chosen for games (Marcus Aurelius over Augustus). Whether another game used them does not matter.
3. **Name the state.** A polity with a capital and a date, not a people, region or ocean ("Abbasid Caliphate", not "Arabia").
4. **Two leaders per nation**, a woman whenever history offers one who really ruled. Each leader carries their own ability; the nation keeps its unique unit and building. The first leader listed is the default.
5. **No living successor polity.** No nation whose successor still governs a people today as a distinct nation (Indigenous nations, reigning traditional monarchies, governments in exile). Modern nation states (England, Japan, Egypt) are fine.
6. **Balance.** No region above about 20%.
7. **Uniques from achievements, not stereotypes.** Chinampas, not sacrifice; the yam relay, not the horde.
8. **Voice** = the leader's nearest living language (EXPERIENCE.md, presentation).

## Wave 2 — the rest (35)

Wave 2 nations are free content, never paid DLC. Selling the non-European nations was a sore point with Civ 6.

**Southeast Asia:** Khmer (Suryavarman II / Jayavarman VII, Khmer) · Ayutthaya (Naresuan / Narai, Thai) · Đại Việt (Lê Lợi / Trưng Trắc, Vietnamese) · Srivijaya (Jayanasa / Balaputradewa, Malay) · Champa (Chế Bồng Nga / Po Romé, Cham)

Across all 51, a woman leads or co-leads 25. Byzantium has none because Irene, its only sole-ruling empress, blinded her son; Joseon's regent Munjeong is remembered for persecutions.

## Removed by rule 5

Haudenosaunee, Cherokee Nation, Hawaiʻi, Tonga, Zulu, Asante (Asantehene reigns), Benin (the Oba reigns), Tibet (government in exile). Replaced by Songhai, Kilwa, Numidia, Liao, Srivijaya, Champa, Mixtec, Chimú.

Cost: Oceania has no nation and North America has only the United States. Places can stand in as wonders (Nan Madol, Cahokia Mounds, Chaco Canyon); Moai are sacred to living Rapa Nui, so check before using them.

## Bench

Netherlands, Sweden, Portugal, Austria, Venice, Gaul, Morocco, Assyria, Armenia (Tigranes), Taungoo (Bayinnaung), Delhi (Razia Sultan), Gupta (Chandragupta II), Maratha (Shivaji), Teotihuacan (Spearthrower Owl). Kievan Rus' (Yaroslav the Wise) is out: Russia and Ukraine both claim it.

## Open

- The engine ships the Civ 5 nations; the roster swap is a ruleset change in `crates/civ-core` data plus leader art and voice.
- Alternate leaders need an ability each (engine: leader abilities split from nation uniques).
- Kilwa and Chimú ship with one leader until a second is found.
- City-state list gets the same regional balance.
